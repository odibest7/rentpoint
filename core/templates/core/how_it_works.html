How It Works

Simple, Transparent Rentals

Browse items, pay securely online, and receive instant digital receipts in three straightforward steps.

1. Browse and choose

Search or filter listings by category, location, and price. Every listing shows the owner's description, condition, and rate.

2. Pay securely

Set your quantity and rental duration, then complete payment through RentPoint's electronic payment channel.

3. Get your receipt

A reference number and dated receipt are generated instantly, and the item owner's earnings update automatically.

For item owners

List an item with a description, category, price, and photos. When a customer pays, your earnings balance updates right away. Request a withdrawal at any time above the minimum threshold, and track the status of each request from your dashboard.
